# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how MedicEdge ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares personal information of users ("you") of our web application.

## Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

*   **Personal Information:** This includes your name, email address, contact number, address, and profile picture. We collect this information when you register for an account, update your profile, or contact us.
*   **Account Information:** This includes your username and email, which are used for authentication and account management.
*   **Webapp Settings:** This includes organization name, slogan, logo, favicon, contact information, and homepage customization settings.
*   **Usage Data:** This includes information about how you use our web application, such as the pages you visit, the features you use, and the time you spend on our platform.
*   **Log Data:** This includes your IP address, browser type, operating system, and other technical information.
*   **Cookies:** We use cookies to store your preferences and track your activity on our web application.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

*   **To Provide and Improve Our Services:** We use your information to operate our web application, personalize your experience, and improve our services.
*   **To Communicate with You:** We use your information to send you emails, notifications, and other communications.
*   **To Protect Our Rights and Interests:** We use your information to protect our rights and interests, such as to prevent fraud and abuse.
*   **To Comply with Legal Obligations:** We use your information to comply with legal obligations, such as to respond to subpoenas and court orders.
*   **Webapp Customization:** To reflect your organization's branding and contact information system-wide.
*   **Promotional Purposes:** To display active users count, healthcare partners count, uptime percentage, and support availability on the homepage.

## How We Share Your Information

We share your information with the following types of third parties:

*   **Service Providers:** We share your information with service providers who help us operate our web application, such as hosting providers, email providers, and analytics providers.
*   **Business Partners:** We may share your information with business partners who offer products or services that may be of interest to you.
*   **Legal Authorities:** We may share your information with legal authorities if we are required to do so by law.
